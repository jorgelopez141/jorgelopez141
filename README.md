I'm a **Data Analytics Manager** with advanced degrees in Business Administration (**MBA, Finance**) and **Data Science (MSc)**. Currently, I am **Internal Audit Data Analytics Manager at Toyota Financial Services** in Plano, Texas, where I build automated SQL/Python pipelines, Tableau dashboards and self-service tools, and lead the division's modernization toward Tableau Cloud, Confluence and GenAI applications.

My background mixes business judgment with technical depth, from credit-risk strategy in Latin America to supply-chain reporting automation and enterprise analytics in automotive finance.
